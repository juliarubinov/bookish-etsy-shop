# Estimated Startup Costs

_2026-10-15. **All estimates**, nothing spent. Sources are third-party unless noted; verify before paying._

## Required
| Item | Estimate | Source / basis |
|---|---|---|
| Etsy one-time shop setup fee | **$15** (some sellers report $19–29) | [LitCommerce](https://litcommerce.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-start-an-etsy-shop/), [Crosslist](https://crosslist.com/blog/etsy-review) |
| Listing fees: 5 × $0.20 | **$1.00** | Etsy fee guides (renews every 4 months or on sale) |
| Printify account (Free plan) | **$0** | [Printify help: Premium changes](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/42641734110225) |
| **Minimum to open** | **≈ $16** (up to $30) | |

## Optional (not recommended at launch)
| Item | Cost | Why wait |
|---|---|---|
| Printify Premium | $39/mo, or $299/yr ([Printify help](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/42641734110225)) | Saves ~$1.72 per tee and ~$4.02 per crewneck (Free vs Premium "from" prices), so it pays off only at about 10–23 items a month |
| Etsy Ads | Your daily cap, e.g. $1–2/day | Better after a few reviews; test small |
| Offsite Ads | $0 upfront; 15% of attributed sales | Charged only on sales; built into the margin scenarios |
| Paid mockup tool | varies | Printify's mockups are free |
