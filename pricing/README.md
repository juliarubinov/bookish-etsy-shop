# Pricing

## Etsy fees (verify current rates before launch)
- Listing fee: $0.20 per listing (renews every 4 months or on each sale)
- Transaction fee: 6.5% of item price + shipping
- Payment processing: ~3% + $0.25 (US; varies by country)
- Offsite Ads: 12–15% on sales from those ads (mandatory above $10k/yr in sales)

## Pricing formula
Price = (materials + packaging + labor + overhead) ÷ (1 − fee % − profit margin %)

A starting rule: aim for **≥ 3× materials cost** for physical goods, and charge
for your time.
