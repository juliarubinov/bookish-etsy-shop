# Shop Concept: Subtle Romance-Reader Humor

_Proposed 2026-10-15. **Approved 2026-10-15:** direction 1, shop name **juliasbookishworld**, printable kept, budget $250. Collection produced (Stage 2). Not published._

## Target customer
_Built from R1–R4 in `research/directions-comparison.md`. Labeled where it's my assumption._
- **Who:** adult romance and romantasy readers, mostly women 20–40 (R3 supports under-35 skew; the upper age is an assumption).
- **Reading life:** reads several romances a month, often more than last year (R2); annotates, tabs, tracks tropes; talks about books online (R4).
- **What they buy here (assumption):** a wearable inside joke that other readers get and strangers don't. Subtle, not explicit. Also gifts for a reading friend or book club.
- **What puts them off (assumption):** loud or explicit slogans, cheap-looking prints, the same five phrases every shop sells.

## Brand positioning
> **For romance readers who want to wear the joke quietly: original, deadpan wording about the
> reading experience, set in editorial type on soft, garment-dyed basics.**

- **Different from:** generic "book lover" shops (we're specifically romance), fandom merch (no series, no characters), explicit "spicy" shops (we're PG and wearable at work).
- **Signature device:** the Department of Yearning, a mock-official office for big feelings (seal, "shifts", "case files").
- **Voice:** dry, affectionate, understated. Fine to wear to the office or to a family dinner.

## Typography (all SIL OFL 1.1, commercial use allowed)
- **DM Serif Display:** headline caps and words
- **Playfair Display Italic (SemiBold):** the emotional word in each phrase
- **Inter SemiBold, tracked caps:** small labels and sub-lines (minimum 0.10 in cap height at print)
- **Lato:** printable body labels
