## Deliberately avoided
- **"Book Boyfriend"**: USPTO serial 99336496, Notice of Allowance issued (education/entertainment classes). Avoided everywhere.
- **"BookTok"**: TikTok platform branding. Not used in products or tags.
- **"One more chapter" / "Just one more chapter"**: heavily used by other sellers; not used.
- Book titles, quotes, characters, authors, series names and franchise imagery: none used.
- Trope names in the printable ("enemies to lovers", "slow burn", etc.) are generic genre terms used
  descriptively. Low risk, but they're on the list for completeness.
- The tags include no other brand names. Blank brand names (Comfort Colors, Gildan) appear only in
  descriptions, to say what the item is printed on.
