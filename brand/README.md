# Brand — Department of Yearning

## Concept
An imaginary office for romance readers. Readers "work" the Night Shift (reading past bedtime) and the
Morning Shift (coffee, then back to the book), carry an "Issued Bag", and file their reading in
"Official Records". It's an original running joke that ties every product together without
borrowing anything from books, authors or fandoms.

## Voice
- Dry, affectionate, in-on-the-joke. Uses official-memo language for big feelings.
- Use: *yearning, shift, issued, records, case file, handle with feelings, on duty*.
- Avoid: book quotes, character or author names, series nods, "BookTok", "book boyfriend", explicit wording.
