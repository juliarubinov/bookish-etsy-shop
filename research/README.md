## Hypotheses (not verified — labeled assumptions)
- **H1** Romance readers buy humorous, "insider" wording about the reading *experience*
  (yearning, slow pacing, staying up late) more than generic "I love books" wording.
  *Basis:* O1, O5, plus how much near-identical generic wording exists (O6). Not
  measured.
- **H2** A cohesive, named concept ("Department of Yearning") will set the shop apart
  from the many shops selling one-off phrase shirts. *Untested.*
- **H3** Comfort Colors 1717 and Gildan 18000 are the blanks bookish buyers expect.
  *Basis:* how often they appear in POD guides; not sales data.
- **H4** Fall/winter (Oct–Feb) favors sweatshirts and mugs; Valentine's Day is a
  romance-specific gifting peak. *Seasonal assumption.*
